# 👻 You've been ghosted!

**Live at [youhavebeenghosted.com](https://youhavebeenghosted.com)**

You scanned a mysterious QR code at a Halloween party. Bold move.
Now you've been ghosted. Don't worry, it's the friendly kind.

## What's inside

- 👻 A very silly ghost who floats around and giggles when you poke it
- 🦇 A few bats, just hanging out
- 😂 **Tell me a spooky joke**: 21 family-friendly groaners, no repeats until you've heard them all
- 🎃 **Pumpkin Hunt**: a pumpkin is hiding behind one of nine gravestones. How fast can you find it?
- 🔊 **Make it spooky**: an optional ghostly "wooOOOooo", made right in your browser (no sound until you ask for it)
- 📤 **Share**: ghost your friends next

## How it's built

One `index.html`. No frameworks, no images, no trackers, no external requests.
The ghost is hand-drawn SVG, the wail is synthesized with the Web Audio API,
and the whole thing is about 14 KB, so it loads fast on party Wi-Fi and cellular alike.
It respects `prefers-reduced-motion`, so the ghost holds still if your phone asks it to.

Hosted on GitHub Pages.

Trick or treat! 🎃
